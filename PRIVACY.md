## 繁體中文

「連撥」是一個完全在你手機本機運作的忙線重撥小幫手。本政策說明本 App 如何處理資料。

### 蒐集的資料項目與類型

本 App **不會蒐集、上傳或傳送任何個人資料到開發者或任何伺服器**。本 App **不連線網路**，也沒有帳號、雲端同步、廣告、分析或追蹤功能。

你在 App 裡自行輸入的電話號碼與備註，以及連撥次數、間隔設定與撥打紀錄，只會存放在這支手機上（WebView 的本機儲存空間）。這些資料不會離開你的裝置。

本 App 不會讀取你的通訊錄，也不需要「直接撥打電話」權限。按下撥號時，會交給手機內建的電話 App 處理，由你確認後再撥出。

### 使用目的

本機儲存的號碼、備註與撥打紀錄，只用來讓你在同一支手機上繼續連撥、查看紀錄與調整設定，沒有其他用途。

### 是否與第三方分享

本 App **不會與任何第三方分享資料**。沒有分析工具、廣告網路、登入服務或雲端服務。

### 資料保存與刪除

資料只會留在這支手機上。你關閉或清除 App 的儲存空間、或解除安裝本 App 後，這些本機資料就會一併刪除。本 App 沒有雲端帳號，因此沒有「刪除帳號」的流程。

### 政策修正時如何通知

若本政策有修改，會更新本頁的「最後更新日期」，並在公開原始碼庫中發布新版本。新版 App 上架時也會一併更新此網址的內容。

### 你的權利

因為資料只存在你的裝置上，你隨時可以在 App 內自行修改或刪除號碼與紀錄，或解除安裝本 App 以刪除全部本機資料。若你對本政策有疑問，請來信：chenluke1975001@gmail.com

## English

Lianbo (連撥) is a busy-line redial helper that works entirely on your device. This policy explains how the app handles data.

### Data collected and data types

The app **does not collect, upload, or transmit any personal data** to the developer or to any server. It **does not use the network**, and it has no accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Phone numbers and notes that you type in the app, together with your redial count, interval settings, and call log, are stored only on this device (in the WebView's local storage). This data never leaves your device.

The app does not read your contacts and does not need the "call phone" permission. When you tap to dial, the call is handed to your phone's built-in dialer so you can confirm it.

### Purposes of use

Numbers, notes, and call logs stored on the device are used only so you can keep redialing, review the log, and change settings on the same phone. They are not used for any other purpose.

### Sharing with third parties

The app **does not share data with any third party**. There are no analytics tools, ad networks, sign-in services, or cloud services.

### Retention and deletion

Data stays only on this device. If you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, the local data is deleted with it. The app has no cloud account, so there is no "delete account" process.

### How you will be notified of changes

If this policy is revised, the "Last updated" date on this page will change and the new version will be published in the public source repository. The same URL will be updated when a new app version is released.

### Your rights

Because the data lives only on your device, you can edit or delete numbers and logs in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. For questions about this policy, email chenluke1975001@gmail.com
